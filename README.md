## About me

I am a **Data Scientist and AI Consultant** specialising in **Modelling and Simulation** and the application of AI to industrial and complex systems. At **Sim4Future**, I work on process optimisation, condition monitoring, predictive maintenance, industrial energy efficiency and data-driven system identification.

I am an **industrial PhD candidate at the University of Genoa (2024–2028, expected)** in collaboration with Sim4Future. My research focuses on *Optimized Process Control Through Enhanced Condition Monitoring*, including the identification of relationships between plant variables from operational data.

My work spans simulation-based optimisation, machine learning, industrial document intelligence and scenario analysis. I have also contributed to international projects involving **NATO and the European Defence Agency (EDA)**, supporting project planning, consortium coordination, technical proposals, work packages and deliverable documentation.

Alongside technical work, I take part in scientific dissemination, university-industry engagement and international conference organisation.

### Selected project contributions

- **Industrial process modelling and optimisation:** Developed Python-based simulation models of plant energy flows to evaluate operating strategies and identify lower-energy set-points for plant engineers.
- **Data-driven system identification:** Researching methods for learning relationships between process variables from operational data without relying on intrusive step tests or plant shutdowns.
- **Maintenance document intelligence:** Developed a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) workflow for extracting maintenance schedules from yacht machinery manuals and querying their content through a chatbot, using Nomic embeddings, ChromaDB, cross-encoder reranking, Ollama and FastAPI.
- **DIMEFIL socio-political simulation:** Developed machine learning forecasting models for socio-economic variables over a five-year horizon within an agent-based simulation framework for analysing conflict scenarios.
- **XR-assisted CBRNe crisis management:** Developed a Unity virtual reality interface for Oculus Rift within a NATO-related extended-reality simulation project.
- **Industrial sensor analytics:** Analysed gas identification sensor records at ABB using SQL and Python to investigate sensor behaviour and support further model development.
